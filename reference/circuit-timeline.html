What happens along the way

Current flows

Gold pulses run along the lit trace from the power connector to the rover.

Chips power on

Each milestone is an IC. When the rover reaches it, the pins, LED and wire to the card light up and sparks fly.

Photos develop

Cover photos start sepia and blurred, then sharpen like a Polaroid once their chip has power.

Stamps land

DEFENDED, VISITED and PASSED stamp onto each card a moment after it develops.

Side components

Resistors and test points along the route light up as current passes them.

Rover details

Wheels turn with distance, the rover tilts on the 45° runs, kicks up dust when you scroll fast and blinks its antenna while moving.

Readout

A seven-segment panel shows the current stage and how much of the board has power.

Finale

The last chip, MTE-2026, lights up and throws confetti. Scroll back up and everything powers down.

Sound, off by default
